### CHEM-000142: Affordable anharmonic lattice free energies
- **Subfield:** Crystallization and molecular solid-state chemistry
- **Open:** Compute converged anharmonic free-energy differences for large libraries of flexible and disordered molecular polymorphs, including finite-size errors, at practical cost.
- **Status:** Normalizing-flow calculations gave accurate free energies for two modeled ice polymorphs in 2025. Diverse molecular crystals remain a broader transfer and scaling challenge.
- **Type:** theory | engineering
- **Why:** Anharmonic entropy can reverse polymorph stability rankings.
- **Key works:** E. Olehnovics et al. (2025), *Accurate Lattice Free Energies of Packing Polymorphs from Probabilistic Generative Models*, [DOI:10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01612](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01612).
